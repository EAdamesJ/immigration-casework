# Example requests

Use invented or properly minimized data when demonstrating the skill.

## Case review

> Use us-immigration-casework to organize the notices I provide. Establish the current agency/stage, label evidence quality, identify contradictions and deadlines, and prepare a concrete next action. Explain which current requirements you could verify.

## Status and time

> Compare the last official status I supply with the documented receipt date. Calculate elapsed calendar time as of the date of analysis. Check the current official processing-time and inquiry tools if available; explain their limits without predicting an approval date.

## Humanitarian preparation

> Prepare a possible expedite package from these facts and documents. Separate the medical evidence from my statements, identify the correct agency/channel, draft the request and an evidence index, and list what the actual clinician must decide or confirm.

## Notice response

> Read the complete notice, extract its exact deadline and submission conditions, map each issue to evidence, and prepare a response outline with visible missing fields. Identify any concrete need for qualified legal review.

## Attorney handoff

> Prepare a concise professional handoff: chronology, notices, unresolved legal questions, urgent dates, actions already taken and their confirmations, and documents still needed. Do not erase contradictions.
