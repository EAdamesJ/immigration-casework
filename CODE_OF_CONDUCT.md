# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect, including people facing stressful immigration situations. Discuss evidence and implementation without personal attacks, harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.

Do not post another person's case, health, family, financial, or identifying information. Do not solicit credentials, promise immigration outcomes, impersonate professionals or authorities, or use issues to market unauthorized services.

Maintain clear, constructive discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful/private material, close discussions outside the project scope, and restrict repeat abusive participation. For a private concern, request a private contact channel without publishing personal details.
