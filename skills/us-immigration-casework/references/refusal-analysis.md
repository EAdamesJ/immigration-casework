# Visa refusal analysis

Start with the actual written notice and its date/post, every cited INA ground, page reference, requested documents, response channel and deadline. Preserve the applicant's interview recollection separately. CEAC “Refused,” slip color, passport retention, officer demeanor or anecdotes do not identify the legal ground or the reason for a decision.

Use `assets/refusal-record.json` and `preparation.py refusal` to route an explicitly transcribed ground. The tool does not read the notice, validate a legal conclusion, compute a deadline or decide eligibility. A `human_reviewed` flag is only a user/host attestation with document/page references. Without original inspection the ground remains unverified. Include all simultaneous grounds; never choose just the easiest one.

| Ground | Review path |
| --- | --- |
| 214(b) | Explain the written nonimmigrant-category/intent basis without inventing the officer's specific reasoning. Compare the prior DS-160, interview facts and genuine changes/new information. DOS describes no appeal process for this refusal and reapplication through a new application/fee/interview; verify current post procedures. Do not recommend an invented mandatory wait, guaranteed strategy, fixed bank balance or scripted false narrative. |
| 221(g) | This is a refusal; distinguish requested documents, administrative processing, or both. Map each requested item to evidence and instructions. DOS describes a one-year information provision rule for missing documentation; verify the notice, dates, current instructions and exceptions before recording a case-specific deadline. Never treat processing as approved or predict a duration. |
| 212(a), multiple, unclear or other grounds | Transcribe the complete subsection. Prepare evidence, chronology and focused questions for a qualified immigration attorney/accredited representative. Do not infer permanence, bars, waiver eligibility, automatic relief or appeal rights. |
| No legible ground | Obtain the complete notice or a clearer copy; continue documenting facts without assigning a statute. |

Deliver: verified ground/source; facts versus hypotheses; old/new information matrix with evidence; outstanding documents; dated next actions; questions for professional review. Do not conceal a prior refusal or classify 221(g) as “never refused” on a new application; read and answer the exact current DS-160 question truthfully. A refusal is not evidence of fraud or wrongdoing by itself. Do not advise litigation or a waiver from this generic module.

Official baseline checked 2026-10-31: [DOS Visa Denials](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/visa-denials.html), sections INA 214(b), INA 221(g), reapplication and confidentiality; [DOS Administrative Processing](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/administrative-processing-information.html). Recheck current sources and the relevant consulate before case-specific recommendations. The baseline is guidance for research, not a frozen statement of future law.
