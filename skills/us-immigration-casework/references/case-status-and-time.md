# Case status and elapsed time

## Verification

Record exact official text, result date, checked timestamp/time zone, channel, source ID, and whether inspected directly. Keep receipts only where needed. Valid format does not prove existence. Distinguish a screenshot from a live result.

Cross-check notices and authorized account documents. Do not substitute undocumented API payloads for a decision notice. Explain statuses in the actual record. A technical timestamp can have no legal significance. Check current USCIS explanations of IOE, SCOPS, time options, and transfers; never guess office, officer, internal checks, queue position, or decision date from a receipt prefix/sequence.

## Stages

Use the supported stage: unknown; USCIS pending/approved; NVC pending/documentarily qualified; interview scheduled; consular review; visa issued; admitted; closed. Complications and parallel applications require separate records or an explicit limitation. Keep history separate from future appointments. Do not open CEAC as though an NVC case already exists without evidence.

## Time analysis

1. Establish documented receipt date and analysis date.
2. Calculate elapsed days and calendar months/remainder. The helper clamps a monthly anniversary to the last valid day; disclose that convention when relevant.
3. Consult the current correct form/category/location combination and its methodology.
4. Separately check official inquiry eligibility; the displayed processing statistic does not establish eligibility to inquire.
5. Explain the historical measurement, limits, and remaining stages.

Do not subtract elapsed time from a percentile to promise a remaining wait. A completion statistic is not approval probability. Do not calculate a legal response deadline with the elapsed-time helper.

When asked what filing date USCIS is working on, establish whether an official comparable indicator exists. NVC's creation/review dates describe NVC work, not a USCIS I-130 queue. Do not manufacture a universal cutoff from social-media examples.

## Failure and follow-up

A 401/403/404, blank page, login wall, missing field, or timeout is an access observation, not a case decision. Record the limitation and use an official alternative. Do not bypass controls or enumerate unrelated cases.

For delays, verify service-request criteria, prior inquiries/waiting periods, delivery, address accuracy, and jurisdiction. Prepare the specific appropriate inquiry. Distinguish expedite, ordinary inquiry, institutional assistance, and a lawyer's litigation evaluation; none guarantees approval or automatically pauses a deadline.
