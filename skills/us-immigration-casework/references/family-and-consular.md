# Family petitions and consular preparation

## Eligibility

Map requirement, authority, fact, evidence, uncertainty, action. Examine petitioner status, relationship, relevant dates, prior marriages, children/age, separate-petition and derivative rules, and material changes. Do not apply one category's quotas to another.

For marriage cases, organize authentic evidence of legal validity and a real relationship: shared life, visits, communication, finances, family, continuity. Missing one type is not automatically disqualification. Do not manufacture a shared story. Separation, divorce, death, abuse concerns, and disputed relationships can alter options and require careful review.

Distinguish petition approval, visa eligibility/issuance, admission, adjustment, and status. Verify the actual rule and relevant moment for CR1/IR1/conditional residence; do not classify from today's date alone. Never attach automatic work/travel permission to a pending petition.

## Sponsorship

Verify current affidavit-of-support instructions: sponsor eligibility, domicile, household/dependents, tax evidence, income, permitted assets, exceptions, joint-sponsor and household-member rules. Distinguish petitioner from financial sponsor. Illness does not by itself establish a financial exception; a relative does not automatically qualify.

Do not hardcode income thresholds, fees, editions, or payments. Prepare a traceable worksheet and flag missing evidence.

## Consular steps

Confirm evidence of each stage before treating it as reached: USCIS decision/transfer; NVC creation/Welcome Letter; authorized secure CEAC access; applicable payments/DS-260; financial/civil documents/translations; documentary review/qualification; post-specific scheduling; authorized medical exam/interview; further documents/processing/decision; issuance; admission/post-arrival steps.

Check the official country reciprocity schedule and consulate instructions. For Dominican Republic, locate current Santo Domingo instructions rather than reusing an old list. Keep Invoice IDs and authentication details out of public reports.

An appointment is not a visa; qualification is not an interview date; one agency's expedite is not automatically accepted by the next. Interpret administrative processing from the record and current guidance. Organize medical evidence without diagnosing or predicting clearance.

## Specialist handoff

For potential inadmissibility, false statements, criminal issues, removal orders, unlawful-presence consequences, waivers, or contested family status, identify the issue and prepare chronology, notices, questions, and missing records for a qualified professional. Do not infer waiver/appeal availability from a category label.
