# Expedite and institutional assistance

## Jurisdiction and criteria

Confirm the agency holding the request. Verify its current criteria, instructions, channel, authorized requester, and follow-up rules. Do not transfer one agency's procedure automatically to USCIS, NVC, or a consulate.

Distinguish urgent humanitarian facts, qualifying financial harm, and other currently recognized criteria. Urgency handling is separate from the underlying benefit decision. Do not guarantee acceptance, timing, or issuance.

## Evidence chain

Map each urgency claim to dated evidence, criterion, connection to the requested action, weakness, and next step. Include chronology, prior requests/responses, and material new evidence; avoid duplicate submissions without reason.

For serious illness, distinguish diagnosis from functional support needs: treatment/appointments, caregiving, household limits, alternatives, separation consequences, and why timing matters. Support financial claims separately.

Prepare a request asking the clinician to consider a letter. The clinician decides the content and must author/review/sign their own opinion. Do not invent prognosis, urgency, inability to work, or clinical necessity of a named person's presence. Obtain the patient's authorization for sharing and include only necessary health information.

## Package

Use the English request and clinician-request templates. Complete only supported facts; leave pending fields visible. Include recipient/channel, requester authority, exact ask, chronology, source-backed criterion, evidence index, existing attachments, and actual authors' signatures. Explain the draft in the user's language.

Assess support and gaps without numerical approval scores. Keep the package unsubmitted until actual authorized submission evidence exists.

## Institutional assistance

Identify a current House representative from the relevant residential address and official district tool; a city may be insufficient. Verify senators, channels, privacy releases, and officeholders on official sources. Prepare a neutral summary, prior inquiry history, and minimum attachments.

Explain the limits of an office's assistance. Verify any ombudsman or service still exists and accepts that request type. Do not promise approval or a suspended deadline. For potential delay litigation, prepare facts/questions for a competent lawyer; elapsed months alone do not establish the appropriate strategy.
