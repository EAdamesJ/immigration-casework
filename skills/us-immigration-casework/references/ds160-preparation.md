# DS-160 preparation and review

Use this workflow for B1/B2 preparation and review. The worksheet is an aide, not the entire conditional CEAC form, an official DS-160 PDF, a submission or a machine import format. For other categories verify their own additional questions and requirements.

1. Establish applicant, consular post, visa purpose, application stage and whether a DS-160 already exists. Inspect existing answers/confirmation when supplied; avoid unnecessary recollection. Never request the security question answer or account secrets in chat.
2. Copy `assets/ds160-worksheet.json` to a private case workspace. Organize identity/native-script name and other names, nationality, passport, contacts/address, itinerary/payer/companions, U.S. contacts, prior travel and refusals, petitions, family, employment/education, social media, security/background and preparer. Add actual conditional fields from the current form; the bundled 23 fields only screen selected preparation gaps.
3. Preserve the exact original name, dates and identifiers with document/page references. Reconcile passport versus prior applications, interview statements, notice grounds and applicant recollection. Do not silently replace an OCR digit or standardize uncertain names. Use the correct field-specific transliteration after applicant review; keep native-alphabet answers in the designated field.
4. Draft English answers with the applicant's actual facts; explain questions in Spanish when useful. Do not infer “No,” “Does Not Apply,” exact dates, employment, income or refusal explanations. Preserve uncertainty and request the missing fact. Use N/A only where the live form permits it and the applicant confirms applicability.
5. Review every current security/background question individually, including conditional follow-ups; a group-level summary is never adequate. Never generate all negative answers from silence. Record the question, supplied response, source and unresolved issue. Flag criminal, immigration violation, misrepresentation and contested admissibility facts for qualified professional review without declaring a legal finding.
6. Run `preparation.py ds160`. Resolve missing answers, applicant confirmations, linked evidence and chronology flags; label the output as selected worksheet review. User-reported facts may remain such even after confirmation. `worksheet_review_complete` is not eligibility, truthfulness verification, readiness to submit, or full CEAC validation. Document references are pointers, not automatic authentication. Review passport-validity exceptions and current post instructions separately.
7. Compare against every live CEAC question and old DS-160, if available. Only then record `ceac_question_by_question_reviewed: true`. Identify any third-party preparer as required. The applicant reviews and electronically certifies/signs; official exceptions for minors or incapacity require verification. The agent does not sign or certify. Preserve the confirmation/barcode page after actual submission and match it against the appointment according to current post instructions.

Current official source baseline checked 2026-10-31: [DOS DS-160 FAQs](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/forms/ds-160-online-nonimmigrant-visa-application/ds-160-faqs.html), sections on English answers, mandatory questions, signing, third-party assistance and confirmation pages. Recheck before case-specific advice. Do not hardcode fees, consular correction procedures, social-media lookback periods or evolving questions from memory.
