# B1/B2 preparation and readiness

Start by establishing the actual temporary purpose, intended dates/duration, destination, payer/budget, occupation/studies, commitments outside the U.S., travel/admission history, U.S. contacts, prior refusals and pending immigrant petitions. Ask only relevant missing questions. Do not assume all nonimmigrant visa categories follow B-visitor rules.

Consult current official visitor instructions, applicable eligibility law and the consulate's instructions. Distinguish B1 business from B2 tourism/visits/medical purposes. Identify activities requiring another category or professional review rather than reshaping the user's true purpose. Keep all facts evidence-labeled. Do not promise issuance or admission.

Use assets/visitor-profile.json as a blank preparation worksheet. Each topic has answer, status and evidence_ids. Record an explicit truthful none/not-applicable answer where appropriate; a blank is unknown. Preserve sensitive answers in private files outside the package. Evidence IDs link the private case ledger, not invented attachments. The helper checks coverage and labels only; the agent must inspect evidence and decide whether a claim is actually supported.

Report complete/partial/incomplete/review_required preparation, strengths supported by evidence, unknowns, contradictions, issues needing professional review and a practical next-action list. Complete preparation does not mean legal eligibility or high approval likelihood. Do not compute approval probabilities, or use marital status, age, nationality, property ownership, accent or wealth as an automatic pass/fail score. Do not treat any single illustrative tie as a mandatory qualification.

Compare statements against available prior applications and documents. Do not invent employment, income, relationships, travel dates, invitations or reasons to return. Identify immigration history and immigrant petitions truthfully; do not infer that their existence either guarantees denial or makes them irrelevant. For fraud, removal, overstays, material inconsistencies or potentially disqualifying history, prepare a qualified-professional handoff.

The v1.1 worksheet is not an official DS-160, automated submission, or a complete admissibility questionnaire. Leave DS-160 field-by-field preparation to the applicable current instructions and future specialized workflow.
