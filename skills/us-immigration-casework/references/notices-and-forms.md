# Notices, forms, decisions, and interviews

## Notice first

Read all pages/attachments and delivery information when relevant. Identify issuer, case/form, exact requested action, grounds, response channel/address, deadline wording, and consequences. Cite notice/page. Distinguish RFE, NOID, NOIR, rejected filing, denial, revocation, 221(g), and court documents.

Never invent a missing due date. Separate an explicit date from a proposed calculation. Verify receipt/mailing rules, zone/cutoff, weekends/holidays, extensions, and current authority. Generic calendar arithmetic cannot settle legal service rules. An inquiry/expedite alone does not establish a paused deadline.

Prioritize near deadlines, detention, and removal issues. Identify the professional review needed while doing independent factual work. Never assume an appeal, motion, or extension exists.

## Response map

Use one row per issue: notice page/requirement, factual answer, evidence ID, gap, draft section. Address every ground without unrelated bulk. Use `assets/templates/notice-response.md` as an outline, not guaranteed legal argument. Check the packet against the notice and current requirements.

## Forms

Select after confirming process/category. I-130/I-130A, DS-260, I-864 variants, I-485, I-765, I-131, I-751, N-400 are examples, not a mandatory bundle.

For each selected form record official page, accepted edition, checked date, fee/payment, channel/address, attachments, signer, preparer/interpreter rules, unresolved fields. Verify volatile items at use. Map answers by part/section/question; follow instructions for blanks, N/A, None, supplements, and signatures. Cross-check history; flag rather than erase discrepancies. Use `[PENDING: specific fact]`; do not mark incomplete forms ready.

Prepare working translations when useful and check certification/signature requirements. Do not claim the AI is a signing translator, attorney, preparer, or applicant. Identify actual assistance and required human review/signatures truthfully.

## Interviews

Ask realistic questions based on the file; explain the issue each clarifies. Help recall/verify true facts, distinguish uncertainty, and resolve actual inconsistencies using authentic evidence. Do not coordinate fabricated answers or conceal material facts.

Keep each declarant's observations separate from speculation/hearsay. Leave declarations for their actual authors' correction/signature. For disputed credibility/admissibility prepare a professional handoff rather than an invented persuasive narrative.
